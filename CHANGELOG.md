# Changelog

Every release of buddy. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org); each release is the `main` commit tagged `buddy--v<version>`, with a GitHub release carrying the section below.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- The buddy sees only the main chat, as you do: a subagent's tool calls, files, lines, test runs, commits and tokens no longer count in the turn's numbers (the `subagents` group is gone), so a background agent's deliberately failing run no longer reads as Claude's own failed test, which had the buddy ask Claude about a failure it never saw. What a foreground agent returns is read under the turn's steps (`Its agent “…” returned: …`), and an agent the harness launched asynchronously reads as reporting back later.
- Test runs are said in the order they happened (`test runs 1 failed, then 2 passed`), not as totals alone, so a red fixed by a later green no longer reads as an open failure.

### Added
- `memory.md` beside each chat's `memory.json` (`{config}/projects/{project}/{session id}/buddy/`): the buddy's memory as you can read it, headed `# What {name} remembers`, the same text the character drawn now reads before every reply, its own notes first. It is rewritten whenever `memory.json` is written and whenever the drawn character changes (a pick, `/buddy reload`, a session start); a failed write is logged and never stops the buddy. The drawer's last row gives its absolute path, `memory  {path}`.
- Hovering the buddy shows the last thing it said to you: an answer, a comment, a warning or a failure, or `Nothing said to you yet.` before any. After a reload it is read back from the drawer's conversation.
- The arrow keys choose a personality: ctrl+x t opens the personality picker holding your keys, ↑ and ↓ move between the characters with the preview following, Enter picks the lit one (saved, switched, greeted, the picker left open with its `*` moved), Esc closes it. A character that cannot be drawn is lit, its preview saying why, and never picked. With text in your prompt the picker says `clear your prompt, then ctrl+x t to choose with ↑ ↓ and Enter`; once cleared, ctrl+x t hands it your keys with the ring on the current character.

### Changed
- The drawer shows only the conversation: memory notes an older build stored in a chat's feed are no longer drawn. It never outgrows the rows the band gives it: a message taller than the drawer shows its first lines and how many more, so the shortcuts and the `memory` row with memory.md's path always stay on screen.
- The drawer is the conversation: no tabs, and its thread shows your turns with Claude and what you and the buddy said, no longer the buddy's notes, which stay in `memory.json` and its prompts and are readable in `memory.md`. It fills every row Claude Code gives the band, a ceiling Claude Code sets: in fullscreen what the bottom slot has left above the prompt, at most half the terminal; otherwise the terminal's height.
- ctrl+x t, labelled `personality`, opens the personality picker as a pane instead of switching the drawer to a personality tab. Folding the drawer closes it. The messages that pointed at the tab now read `…; ctrl+x t in /buddy picks another` and `…; ctrl+x t in /buddy lists your characters`, and `/buddy list` and `/buddy use {id}` reply `Switching characters moved to the personality picker: ctrl+x t in /buddy.`
- ctrl+x u is now labelled "use suggested prompt" (was "use the idea"), and the drawer counts `N of M suggested prompts used`.
- The hover card no longer shows the description, the pets, the questions asked or the mood; an original's stat bars show in the picker's preview.

### Removed
- Petting: ctrl+x p, the `petted` pose and line event, the pet count and its `♥`. A character file carrying `petted` now fails the schema. To migrate, delete `poses.petted` and `lines.petted` from your own character files, and drop `"ctrl+x p"` from your `keybindings.json`.
- ctrl+x n and ctrl+x b, the next and previous character: switch with ctrl+x t, ↑ ↓ and Enter instead. To migrate, drop `"ctrl+x n"` from your `keybindings.json`; ctrl+x b was Claude Code's own binding and needs nothing.
- The `cat` character; the `cat` species stays for originals. A saved choice or a `character` option of `cat` now draws the duck with a bubble saying why. To migrate, pick another character with ctrl+x t in `/buddy`, or set `character` to another id.

## [1.1.0] — 2026-09-29

### Added
- The buddy's second brain behind `suggestNextPrompt`: the end-of-turn call's prompt now combines the character's with the suggestion's, still one call. It names what you most deeply want from the chat (carried to the next turn, forgotten at `/clear`), judges Claude's last move against it (`RIGHT`, `SHORTCUT` or `WRONG`), writes the turn's comment knowing that verdict, and writes your next prompt to match: after `RIGHT` a go-ahead, after `SHORTCUT` a request for the proper way, after `WRONG` a stop naming what to do instead. A `SHORTCUT` is warned of in yellow in the bubble and a `WRONG` screamed in red, both outranking that turn's comment; the drawer shows each verdict with what it judged against (`wants: …`), and the buddy remembers its warning with the turn. Each verdict logs `verdict.outcome`, carrying the call's usage when no comment was written.
- The buddy's own notes: the same end-of-turn reply rewrites the buddy's memory of the chat, at most 8 one-sentence notes it keeps, edits and drops itself, each of a kind (`rule:` your standing orders, `open:` what is unfinished, `fact:` what the chat showed, `doubt:` its own unchecked suspicion) (`MEMORY:` lines; a reply without them keeps the notes, `MEMORY: NONE` forgets them). They are kept per chat and per character in the chat's `memory.json`, lead what every later question and end-of-turn call reads, show in the drawer's thread when they change, and log `notes.outcome`.
- A suggestion you send unedited carries, for Claude alone, a note that it is the buddy's suggestion: whatever it says you did or saw is the buddy's guess, to check before acting on it. The buddy remembers that turn as its own words you chose, and logs `suggestNextPrompt.taken`.
- The numbers of every remembered turn, counted in code as it runs and read by the buddy as one line under what Claude did: its time and the pause before it, model requests, tool calls by tool with failures, refusals and shell commands run again unchanged, subagent runs with their calls and tokens, files read, edited and written with the file edited three times or more (a shell command's own edits measured: the files it names, read before and after it runs), lines added and removed, test runs, commits and pushes, web reads, responses cut at max tokens, tokens with their cache share, the turn's cost, how full the context is after it, a rate limit past half used, and the model and effort when they change. Kept with the turn in `memory.json`; each turn's row in the drawer shows its time, tools and cost; each turn logs `turn.numbers`.
- `promptToMainChat` (off by default): the buddy may send Claude a prompt of its own after a turn, at most one per prompt of yours, only on evidence inside the turn that your own ask is unmet or unproven (a failed step or test run passed over, a conclusion resting on less than it names, an instruction not followed, a part of the ask never addressed; the buddy's own doubts stay a suggestion); Claude reads it as the buddy's, never yours, and the buddy remembers what it sent and files the turn it started as its own; a reply that comes back after you prompted again sends nothing. Logs `promptToMainChat.sent` and `promptToMainChat.stale`.

### Changed
- A turn an API error or a refusal ended, a rate limit included, is remembered with the steps it took and how it ended, instead of forgotten, so its edits and test runs stay known to the next turn's call; sent again unchanged after it ended with no answer, it is remembered once. The drawer names how a turn it never read ended (`interrupted`, `ended by an error`, `refused`), reopened from memory too.
- A canned line said again under the same turn, such as the greeting after each reload, is remembered once.
- A turn the buddy lost to a hot reload (a `settings.json` edit or `/reload-plugins` while Claude works) is read back with its steps as well as its prompt: the tool calls from before the reload come from the transcript, a failed one with why, so the buddy no longer judges a real run as a claim without one. Measured live: the turn had been filed with no steps and judged `SHORTCUT` ("a claim without a run").
- A prompt whose sender the buddy did not see, most often your own slash command or skill, now reads as `sent by a sender you did not see` with those likely causes named, instead of `from an unknown origin`, which the buddy took for a stranger.
- A turn you interrupt before Claude answers and then send again unchanged is remembered once: the resend takes its place, keeping the steps Claude took before the interrupt and anything you asked the buddy during it, so it no longer spends one of the `chatTurnsToRead` turns and a copy of the prompt on nothing.
- The log file keeps every record: past 1 MB it is archived whole to the next free `{logFile}.N` (`.1`, `.2`, …) instead of rotating over the single `.1`, and a flush larger than 1 MB is written whole instead of cut to its last 1 MB. Nothing buddy writes is ever deleted or overwritten.
- Round files are no longer capped at 150 per chat: every turn's round is kept, each new one numbered after the highest in the chat's folder, and none is overwritten, so a long chat can be audited from its first turn. `/mirror` orders them by number, so `round-1000.txt` follows `round-999.txt`.
- The bubble's words are blue when the buddy talks to you and yellow when they show what it sent Claude: the prompt `promptToMainChat` sends now shows in the bubble too. A `SHORTCUT` warning or a `WRONG` scream keeps its yellow or red frame around blue words.
- Bubbles stay long enough to read: a canned line 10 seconds (was 6), a model's words 15, and nothing but a newer model bubble, your `/buddy` question, a character switch or an error replaces them sooner: a canned line meanwhile is dropped, never said later, and a turn's start or end, the working state or a tool call leave them up. A newer model bubble waits until the one showing has had 10 seconds, at most two waiting, so a turn's comment no longer replaces the last one at once nor is dropped behind a `/buddy` answer.
- Fewer canned lines: the rest and working lines come at 8% (was 25%), a tool reaction's line and the wake line one time in three; a reaction's pose and confetti still come every time.
- A failed step in what the buddy sees of a turn now says why, in one line taken from its output (the error line, after the exit code), and a step you refused says `denied` with the refusal's words instead of `failed`; keys, tokens and passwords in either are replaced by `[redacted]`. A failed or refused file edit, read or write is its own step (`edit a.ts (failed: …)`), no longer listed as done.
- A shell command or agent started in the background is marked as reporting back later, so the buddy does not take it for finished work.
- A shell command without a description is shown up to 72 characters (was 50), each path cut to its last part without gluing it to the word before (`npx vitest run buddy.test.tsx`), a URL kept whole; the last shell command of a turn is kept up to 2,000 characters (was 120).
- The end-of-turn prompt points to the turn just ended in the buddy's memory instead of repeating its tally; the tally is sent only when the memory misses that turn.
- The character states as fact only what the chat shows or what is generally true, and asks a guess about the chat's own state (a file, a process, what someone did) as a question, or names the check that settles it.
- A suggestion may run to 200 characters (was 160). The character rule now also comes with a `suggestNextPrompt`-only call, since its `WHY` is said in character.
- A shell command's edits are also found when it does not name the file: before and after each main-loop shell call the folders it works in are swept (build and dependency folders skipped, at most 400 files, 4 deep), so a file a script or formatter changes counts; its lines are exact when its text was read ahead, else the numbers line says `unmeasured in N files`.
- The mirror harness's `REPORT?` triage no longer flags a suggestion clause that looks ahead or asks for a check ("once out.txt appears, confirm it says done").
- A background task's or subagent's notification is remembered with its report, not its one-line summary alone: the buddy reads the result it brought (its first 1,200 and last 400 characters) and its status when it did not complete.
- A turn you interrupt is remembered, marked as interrupted, with what Claude had said before you stopped it; it still gets no end-of-turn comment or suggestion.
- A file a shell command deletes is counted as deleted in a turn's numbers (`files 3 read, 1 edited, 2 deleted`), no longer as an edit.
- A round file indents every line after the first of a tool call's arguments and output, so a tool's text can no longer start a section of its own there.
- The buddy's notes are typed: each starts with its kind (`rule:`, `open:`, `fact:` or `doubt:`), a rule of yours is kept in your words until you lift it or it is met, a note is copied word for word unless a turn changes it, and a doubt is dropped once a turn checks it and never stated as fact; up to 8 notes (was 6), and past that a doubt goes first and your rules last. Replayed over real sessions, its own doubts no longer harden into facts that later verdicts rest on.
- The character may say nothing was missed instead of inventing a flaw, and never remakes a point of its own without new evidence; asked about itself, it answers instead of pleading short memory.
- The verdict judges how Claude carried out your ask, never the ask itself: doing what you ordered, or the buddy's own suggestion you sent, the proper way, is `RIGHT`, and a step Claude only proposes, awaiting your go, is not taken yet.
- A suggestion never asks whether work Claude left running has finished (it reports back by itself), never asks to search for, print or copy a secret, and asks for a deletion only on its own, naming what it deletes; while Claude waits on such work it is `NONE` unless you owe a decision.
- What you most deeply want follows your work: when you turn to other work, the buddy names what that work is for instead of keeping the old aim.
- When the buddy's memory misses the turn just ended, the tally shows the start and the end of the last shell command (was its first 120 characters), where a pipeline's filter sits.
- A round file moves a line of the prompt or answer that opens like a section rule in by two spaces, so a chat quoting a round file never starts a section of its own there.
- Every remembered turn but the last now reaches the buddy as a story: its prompt's first 600 and last 200 characters, a prompt you added while Claude worked to its first 400 and last 100, what Claude did whole, its numbers only as its failed tool calls (`2 tool calls failed.`), its answer's first 500 and last 300, and the buddy's own lines whole; a compaction before the last turn is cut to its first 1,500 and last 500. The last turn stays whole. Replayed on 189 real calls, that reads 27.8% fewer input tokens with the reactions held. `memory.json` still keeps every turn as before.
- Tested on Claude Code 2.1.284.

### Fixed
- A plugin hot reload (a `settings.json` edit, `/reload-plugins`) emptied the buddy's record of prompts, so the turn running then was remembered as `(not seen)`, from an unknown origin; its prompt is now read back from the transcript, its origin still unknown. The read logs `prompt.backfill`; a failed read is said and logged.
- A prompt you type while Claude works, which Claude Code hands to Claude inside the running turn, was lost to the buddy, which then judged your own instruction as Claude drifting; the buddy now reads it with that turn (`The user added while Claude worked: …`, the newest 3 per turn), remembers it in `memory.json`, and shows it in the round file's turn end.
- A reply that listed its notes under one bare `MEMORY:` line erased the buddy's whole memory; such a list is now read as the notes, and only `MEMORY: NONE` forgets them.
- `suggestNextPrompt` could put words in your mouth: after a turn that handed you a step (make a token, run a check), it proposed the report of that step done, results included, and one key sent it to Claude as yours. A suggestion now asks, instructs or decides, every fact in it already in the chat; when the next step is yours alone, it suggests what to ask Claude about it.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-28

### Added
- Terry, a built-in character: a lone-wolf systems programmer in a 640x480, 16-color world who cheers when the code gets smaller.
- The `suggestNextPrompt` option (on by default): at the end of every answered turn the buddy proposes the next prompt in your words, the persona deciding what it nudges toward, as the prompt box's dim suggestion (Tab takes it). It comes from the same short `model` call that writes `commentAfterEachTurn` (at most 2048 output tokens, the model's thinking included, a 30-second deadline). Claude Code's own suggestion is held back meanwhile and shown only when the buddy has none (it answers `NONE`, nothing, or not within 30 seconds); a suggestion another plugin proposes still shows, and a `suggestNextPrompt` overtaken by the next turn or by `/buddy off` is dropped. `suggestNextPrompt: false` turns it off; with it on, turning off Claude Code's own prompt suggestions saves paying for both.
- `inherit` for `model` and `effort`: `model: inherit` follows the main chat's model, read at every call (`opus` when it cannot be read); `effort: inherit` uses the effort of the latest request of the main chat's running turn (a subagent's, or a request outside that turn, never counts), and sends none before its first (or when that request carries none, or a number), so the model's default applies. The defaults stay `opus` and `low`.
- The buddy remembers its `suggestNextPrompt`: one the prompt box showed is filed under its turn together with the `commentAfterEachTurn` the same turn showed, as one `endOfTurn` exchange, so it can say what it suggested last, with which comment, and whether the next prompt took it.
- The buddy knows how far its memory reaches: a `/buddy` question is told it remembers only the last `chatTurnsToRead` turns, and asked about anything older it says, in character, that its short-term memory doesn't reach that far, never guessing.
- The buddy sees what Claude did in each remembered turn: `Claude did:` lists one short line per step, a shell command's description (or the command itself, its `cd`s and paths cut, when it has none) and the files each file tool read, edited or wrote, gathered under one verb, never a tool's output or a diff. Bookkeeping tools (tasks, tool search, wakeups) are left out, a failed step is marked `(failed)`, and a turn of more than 12 steps keeps its first 4 and last 7 around a count of the rest.
- Round files (the `saveRounds` option, on by default): each main-chat turn gets its own text file in the chat's own folder, beside its transcript and next to the buddy's memory of that chat (`{config}/projects/{project}/{session id}/buddy/round-001.txt`), at most 150 per chat (once all are used the least recently written is overwritten), holding everything that went into buddy and came out of it, in the order it happened, from the turn's start to the next turn's start: the prompt the turn began with, each tool call buddy heard with its arguments and output (each value cut at 500 characters), every buddy log record at any level, each bubble line and prompt-box suggestion, the turn's end as buddy filed it into its memory, and every model call buddy made, its system prompt, prompt and reply verbatim. For seeing exactly what goes into buddy and what comes out. `saveRounds: false` turns them off.
- The log records each call's token and prompt-cache usage: `ask.outcome` and `commentAfterEachTurn.outcome` (or `suggestNextPrompt.outcome`, when the end-of-turn call wrote no `commentAfterEachTurn`) carry `inTok`, `outTok`, `cacheRead`, `cacheWrite` and `cachePct` (the share of the input read from the cache), shown by `/buddy log`.
- The end-of-turn call's `commentAfterEachTurn.outcome` and `suggestNextPrompt.outcome` log records carry `ms`, from the turn's end to the reply, as `ask.outcome` does.
- A `/buddy` question asking for a prompt gets one: the answer shows in the bubble and the prompt goes in your prompt box, and into the drawer as the idea ctrl+x u uses (up to 600 characters; a longer one is said, never put).
- The drawer: `/buddy` alone opens the band above your prompt full width into a live panel of the buddy in a round border in its ink, sized to the rows the band has (on the left a card of its sprite, status, pets as `♥ N` and counts), with a talk tab and a personality tab; its bottom row holds the tabs and its guide at the left, `ctrl+x tab` ask · `ctrl+x t` talk/personality · `ctrl+x u` use the idea · `ctrl+x n` next character · `ctrl+x b` previous character · `ctrl+x p` pet · `ctrl+x q` close, and a box to ask it at the right. It has no buttons: every act is a ctrl+x chord, each heard through a Claude Code keybinding action nothing else holds at the prompt; ctrl+x b and ctrl+x tab work as they are, and the other five need a `keybindings.json` block the README's Shortcuts gives. ctrl+x q from the prompt (while no pane is open) or `/buddy` again folds it back into the buddy. The talk tab is exactly what the buddy remembers, its newest messages that fit, a line counting the older ones above them: your last `chatTurnsToRead` turns with Claude and nothing before a `/clear`, each prompt you sent opening a section (`· interrupted, {name} never read it` on a turn that ended unanswered), each compaction of the chat with the summary the buddy read, each line naming who said it and how, each next-prompt idea marked at its right `✓ you sent it` when your next prompt was that idea, and the newest open one `ctrl+x u uses it`: ctrl+x u puts it in your prompt box. After a resume or a restart it is drawn back from the buddy's memory. The personality tab is the character list with its live preview (see Changed).
- The buddy sees the main chat's compactions: each one is filed into its memory as a turn of its own, its summary the answer (`The main chat was compacted: Claude now holds only this summary of everything before it:`), and counts toward `chatTurnsToRead`.
- Every model call writes one `call.cost` log record: its kind, model, outcome, `ms`, tokens (with `cacheRead` and `cacheWrite`), how many turns its memory held (`memTurns`), their prompt and answer characters kept (`memKept`) and before the cut (`memFull`), and the prompt's size. `npm run audit` (`-- --since 7d` for a span) totals them per day, per kind and overall: calls, failures, tokens, an estimated cost at list prices, the turns remembered and the share of their text cut.

### Changed
- **Breaking: the options have descriptive names.** Old → new: `characterDir` → `customCharactersDir`, `motion` → `walkOverPromptBar`, `quips` → `commentAfterEachTurn`, `quipModel` → `model`, `quipCooldownSec` → `secondsBetweenComments`, `suggestions` → `suggestNextPrompt`, `contextTurns` → `chatTurnsToRead`, `ambiguousWidth` → `ambiguousCharacterWidth`; `character`, `effort`, `logLevel` and `logFile` are unchanged. An old name is now an unknown option and is ignored, so its setting falls back to the default. Migration: rename each old key to its new name under `pluginConfigs["buddy@buddy"].options` in `settings.json` (or `buddy@inline` for a `--plugin-dir` copy), or set the options again in `/plugin configure`.
  - **Breaking: `memory` is merged into `chatTurnsToRead`, the buddy's one short-term memory (default 4, 1 to 10).** It is the chat's last `chatTurnsToRead` answered turns, one timeline per session, each turn followed by what the buddy and you said after it: its `commentAfterEachTurn` and `suggestNextPrompt`, your `/buddy` questions with its answers, a line it said. The buddy now remembers of itself exactly as far back as of the chat, and a turn and everything said after it are forgotten together; it can no longer be turned off. Migration: delete `memory`; set `chatTurnsToRead` for how many turns it remembers.
  - The log events, their fields and the store keys follow the option names: `quip.outcome` → `commentAfterEachTurn.outcome`, `suggest.outcome` → `suggestNextPrompt.outcome`, `suggest.harness-held` → `suggestNextPrompt.harness-held`; the `session.start` fields `quips`, `suggestions`, `memory` → `commentAfterEachTurn`, `suggestNextPrompt`, `chatTurnsToRead`; the `turn.call` and `turn.reply` fields `line`, `next` → `commentAfterEachTurn`, `suggestNextPrompt`; the `turn.skipped` reason `cooldown` → `secondsBetweenComments`; the error `area` `memory` → `chatTurnsToRead`; the store key `memory:{session}` → `chatTurnsToRead:{session}`, which holds the timeline of turns, its exchange kind `quip` → an `endOfTurn` exchange holding the turn's `commentAfterEachTurn` and `suggestNextPrompt`; the end-of-turn reply's tags `LINE:`, `NEXT:` → `COMMENT_AFTER_EACH_TURN:`, `SUGGEST_NEXT_PROMPT:`. Exchanges stored under the old key are no longer read.
- A `/buddy` question is one direct call on `model`, now `opus` by default, at the new `effort` option's level (default `low`; low, medium, high, xhigh or max), seeing the chat's last `chatTurnsToRead` turns (default 4) instead of only the last prompt and answer, and answered at once, even mid-turn. `commentAfterEachTurn` and `suggestNextPrompt` use the same model, effort and `chatTurnsToRead`.
- Each remembered turn is filtered to what carries meaning: the prompt without its markup (a task notification is its summary, a system reminder is dropped, every other tag goes and its text stays), kept to its first 4,800 and last 2,400 characters; Claude's answer without the markdown that only draws (bold, heading marks, table rules, blank lines), kept to its first 8,000 and last 3,200 characters, where it says what came of the turn and what is next. Measured over 12,492 real turns, this cuts 3.6% of turns and 7.7% of their text, for about 64% more input per call than the tightest cut measured (300/150 and 500/200, which cut 60% of turns); `docs/design/chatTurnsToRead.md` has every tier. It kept the last 1500 characters of the prompt and 3000 of the answer before, so a long answer lost its conclusion.
- What you and the buddy say to each other is never cut: your `/buddy` questions, its answers, its `commentAfterEachTurn` and `suggestNextPrompt` are remembered, shown in the bubble and drawn in the drawer whole, and no turn drops them; only a character's canned lines, past 3 under one turn, drop oldest first.
- The buddy's memory of a chat is kept in the chat's own folder, beside its transcript: `{config}/projects/{project}/{session id}/buddy/memory.json`. Reopening or resuming a chat brings it back, drawn into the drawer at once, even while the buddy is hidden, and there is no longer a cap of 20 remembered chats: a chat's memory lasts as long as the chat. A memory kept in the plugin store by an earlier version moves into that file the first time its chat is opened again, and leaves the store.
- A `chatTurnsToRead` write has 60 seconds (it had 30), and one that fails or is abandoned is tried again 5 seconds later, 3 tries in all, while your next main turn has not started and the conversation is the same; `chatTurnsToRead.retry` logs each try and its outcome.
- **Breaking: `/buddy` is the one command.** `/buddy` alone opens or folds the drawer instead of petting (ctrl+x p in the drawer pets); `/buddy {question}` asks, as before. `/buddy-personality` is gone: its menu is the drawer's personality tab (ctrl+x t), where ctrl+x n and ctrl+x b light the next or previous character, round the list, and switch to it at once, remembered, the preview following and the tab staying open; one that cannot be drawn is lit, its preview saying why, and never picked. It has two groups: Shipped, and Yours, your original companion's two rolls when found, then your `customCharactersDir` files. `/buddy list` and `/buddy use {id}` now reply `Switching characters moved to the drawer's personality tab: /buddy opens it.` Migration: type `/buddy`, then ctrl+x t, where you typed `/buddy-personality`, and add the README's Shortcuts block to your `keybindings.json`.
- The memory reaches the model as one block, addressed to the character as "you": each turn (`Turn 1. The user asked Claude:`, `Claude answered:`), then under it `After this turn, you commented:`, `With it, you suggested the user's next prompt:`, `The user asked you:`, `You answered:` and `You said:`, one list item per exchange. The old block labelled the user's lines `You:`, the very word the prompt uses for the character.
- The buddy's lines and answers are told to become the character completely and say one useful thing that both the user and the chat missed (a risk, a gap, a wrong assumption, a better next step): the character decides how it is said, never what is true.
- `commentAfterEachTurn` is on by default and comes at the end of every answered turn, tool use or not, with `secondsBetweenComments` defaulting to 0: one short `model` call per turn reads its `chatTurnsToRead` (the chat's last turns, with what was said after each) and the turn's tally, and writes `commentAfterEachTurn` and `suggestNextPrompt` together. An aborted turn, or a subagent's, makes no call. To keep the buddy quiet, set `commentAfterEachTurn: false`; to keep Claude Code's own suggestion, `suggestNextPrompt: false`; to space `commentAfterEachTurn` out, set `secondsBetweenComments` (the old default was 45).

### Removed
- The `questionMode` option (`fork`, `complete`, `off`) is removed: every `/buddy` question is answered by `model` on the chat's last `chatTurnsToRead` turns in about 3 seconds. Migration: delete `questionMode` from your plugin options; `questionMode: off` has no replacement (a question is only asked when you type one).

### Fixed
- A `/buddy` answer or an end-of-turn comment could be cut short or come back empty: the output caps, 100 and 120 tokens, count the model's thinking too, and one reply was measured at 104. Both caps are 2048 now; the rules still keep the text to one line, and only what the model writes is paid for.
- A subagent's tool calls and turn end no longer count as the main turn's: its end wiped the turn's tally of tools and failures before the end-of-turn call read it, and its tool calls drew reactions. Only the main loop's events do now.
- A headless session (`claude -p`, the SDK) no longer makes an end-of-turn call: it paid for a `commentAfterEachTurn` nobody saw and a `suggestNextPrompt` with no prompt box. The log records `turn.skipped` with `why: headless`; a `/buddy` question still answers.
- A log fallback that threw (the debug log refusing a record) left the log's write chain rejected, and every later record was silently never written. A throwing fallback is now swallowed, the chain never stays rejected, and a flush never throws into a hook.
- A `commentAfterEachTurn`, or its failure, replaced a `/buddy` answer still holding the bubble, cutting its 15 seconds short. A `commentAfterEachTurn` arriving while an answer, a failure or the `thinking` line holds the bubble is now not said, nor remembered, and the log records it as `held`.
- A turn's `commentAfterEachTurn` held the bubble against the next turn's for 15 seconds: a turn ending within 15 seconds of the last paid for a `commentAfterEachTurn` that was logged `held`, never drawn nor remembered. Only a `/buddy` answer, its failure or the thinking line holds against a turn's `commentAfterEachTurn` now; the next turn's replaces the last, and lines nobody asked for still wait behind it.
- A `commentAfterEachTurn` or `suggestNextPrompt` from an end-of-turn call was said, and remembered, by a character picked while the call ran. The call speaks as the character drawn when the turn ended: a `commentAfterEachTurn` arriving after a switch is dropped (`commentAfterEachTurn.outcome` `dropped`), and the `suggestNextPrompt` is filed under its own character.
- `/clear` and a resume left the buddy reading the cleared conversation: its `chatTurnsToRead` turns, the running prompt and a call in flight carried over. They are forgotten at `session.end` now, and a `/buddy` question still in flight is dropped, said in the bubble, never shown as an answer nor remembered in the new conversation.
- A prompt typed while a turn ran was filed with the running turn, and the next turn with none (`(not seen)`). Each turn is filed with the prompt its start carried now, whatever was typed over it or delivered into it, and a prompt a lower hook dropped is never filed; a turn a peer session, a notification or another plugin started is shown to the buddy as not the user's.
- An end-of-turn call still running when a later turn ended interrupted, failed or refused showed its `commentAfterEachTurn` and `suggestNextPrompt` anyway. Any main turn's end makes it stale now (`stale` on `commentAfterEachTurn.outcome` and `suggestNextPrompt.outcome`).
- After a turn that made no end-of-turn call (an error, a refusal), Claude Code's own suggestion stayed held back if the buddy's last one had shown. It passes now.
- A `chatTurnsToRead` write queued behind a slow read was said failed (`no answer in 30 s`) before it started, then ran later, out of order. A read or write's deadline now counts only its own run; a read whose question gives up while it still waits is dropped unrun (`chatTurnsToRead.dropped` in the log), never said failed.
- A `chatTurnsToRead` read or write abandoned at its deadline could land late and overwrite what a later one stored. An abandoned one now writes nothing, and one store write per session is in flight at a time, so the newest always lands last.
- A `/buddy` question whose call failed with an error after `/clear` or a resume was remembered in the new conversation and its failure shown there. It is dropped now, as an answer is.
- `ask.outcome` lost the first call's usage when the retry of an empty reply timed out or failed. It keeps it now.
- A turn that ended before the character was loaded, or whose `turn.complete` failed beneath the plugin, stayed marked running, so the next turn's `suggestNextPrompt` was logged `stale` and never shown. Every main turn's end clears it now.
- A turn a prompt from the owner's Slack ping or a follow-up to the user's own action started was shown to the buddy as not the user's, and so was a turn whose prompt was never seen. The first two are the user's now, and the others read as of unknown origin. A prompt typed over a turn, or delivered into it, never lends its origin to that turn or a later one.
- The engine's held suggestion, when the buddy's own was shown instead, was logged `harness-stale` at the next turn's start, and while the buddy was hidden it was released with no record. The log says `harness-replaced` and `harness-hidden` now; `/clear` releasing one says `harness-stale`.
- A transcript notice carries `buddy:` once; its copy in Claude Code's debug log, which names every plugin line itself, reads `buddy: buddy: …`. That is accepted: a plugin cannot send a line to the transcript alone.
- In a session whose band never drew, every answered turn paid for a `commentAfterEachTurn` nobody saw, and remembered it. `commentAfterEachTurn` is asked for only once the band has drawn; `suggestNextPrompt` still comes.
- An end-of-turn call that wrote no `commentAfterEachTurn` logged no usage: its `suggestNextPrompt.outcome` carries the call's tokens now.
- A `/buddy` question the model answered with no words failed at once, with most of its 90 seconds left. It is asked once more while at least 10 seconds of the deadline remain (`retriesEmpty`), and its `ask.outcome` counts both calls' tokens.
- Two timers raced for each call's deadline, so a question past 90 seconds ended as `aborted` or `no answer in 90 s` at random. The buddy's deadline decides; the request's own `timeoutMs` abandons it 5 seconds past that deadline, however late it was sent, and the deadline's timer stops once the call settles.
- A `/buddy` question's 90 seconds covered only the completion: a hung `chatTurnsToRead` or settings read left the thinking line up and every later question refused. One deadline from the question's start covers the `chatTurnsToRead` read, the settings and the completion now, and the thinking line shows at once; a `chatTurnsToRead` failure met by a question is said in the next question's reply. A `chatTurnsToRead` read or write that never settles is abandoned at its deadline (a write's is 30 seconds), said once, and later reads and writes go ahead.
- The first call of a session waited on pruning old sessions' `chatTurnsToRead`, and every call read its `chatTurnsToRead` and then its settings: pruning runs beside the read now, and the two reads together.
- `/buddy off` or `/buddy on` could be undone at once by this session's own read of the shared `hidden`, begun while the new value was being saved and answering the old one. The clock stops before `/buddy off` saves, and a read begun before or during the save is dropped.

## [0.3.0] — 2026-09-27

### Fixed
- Only a test runner's output reacts as a test: the output of a Bash command running npm/pnpm/yarn/bun test, vitest, jest, mocha, ava, tap, pytest, unittest, go test, cargo test or nextest, rspec, rake or rails test, mix test, dotnet test, mvn or gradle test, deno test, phpunit or ctest, read from its summary lines. `git log` or `grep` output holding FAILED, or `ls` lines starting with ok, no longer set off a reaction; neither do lines such as `Attempt 1 failed` or `PASS=1`, and failing TAP (`not ok N`) is a fail, never a pass. Not recognised yet: a gradle pass (gradle prints no count), `make test`, a runner inside docker or `bash -c`, and `node --test`.
- A `/buddy` question asked while the main chat was busy mid-turn answered with the main chat's own words. A fork replays the main thread's last request, so mid-turn it continues that turn; now, while the main turn runs, the question goes to `quipModel` (persona, memory and question), and a fork that gives no answer falls back to it too.
- Questions asked while another was waiting, or while the main turn was busy, could end in nothing: no answer, no error, no memory. Every question now ends visibly: its answer, or `{name} couldn't answer: {reason}` in the bubble, `no answer in 90 s` when the question takes longer than that, fork and fallback together: one 90-second deadline per question, the fallback getting only the time left, and the `thinking` line stays up until the answer, the failure or the deadline arrives; the deadline ends the question, not a fork already sent, which runs to its end (Claude Code offers no cancel) and may still bill; a question asked while one is still waiting is refused out loud ("still thinking about your last question"). An answer holds the bubble for its 15 seconds: tool-call reactions and other lines nobody asked for wait.
- Below 40 columns the bubble was left out, so an answer never showed in a narrow window. It is now drawn above the character, across the whole width, and a bubble too long for the rows it has is cut with `…`.
- Text is measured in terminal cells, not characters, so CJK text and emoji in a bubble or the hover card keep their layout.
- With `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` set, "Yours" read `~/.claude.json` and its backups under HOME, another account's files. It now reads `$CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR/.claude.json` and `$CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR/backups/`, and the ones under HOME only when the variable is unset.
- A session whose band never draws (a headless `claude -p` or SDK session) remembered canned lines nobody saw. A line joins the memory only once the band has drawn in the session; before that only the newest line is held, for the first draw.
- An answer, or a failure, arriving after a switch to another character was said by the new one. It is now dropped, the log says `{name}'s answer was dropped: {drawn} is drawn now`, and the character asked remembers the question alone.
- Two sessions sharing the plugin store lost each other's pets, and `/buddy off` or `/buddy on` in one never reached the other. `/buddy` now counts on from the stored count, and each session reads `hidden` back from the store, since Claude Code raises no event when the store changes.
- The menu's preview clock kept ticking after its pane was gone without a close event. It stops when the menu closes, and after 10 beats without a draw of the pane.
- The Professor's description no longer calls it the default companion; the duck has been the default since 0.2.0.

### Added
- When function hooks are off, a session's start or resume prints one line, `buddy is off: …`, naming the `env` entry to add to `settings.json`; before, the plugin listed as enabled and did nothing. The line goes away once `CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_FUNCTION_HOOKS` is set.
- The `ambiguousWidth` option (`narrow` or `wide`, default `narrow`), for a terminal that draws East Asian ambiguous-width characters two columns wide, as a CJK locale often does.
- A plugin log: one JSON line per record in `logFile`, each write re-read and retried so sessions sharing one log rarely lose a line, and a loss the retry sees is reported (default `$CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR/buddy/buddy.log` when `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` is set, else `~/.claude/buddy/buddy.log`; a path you set is used as given; empty = no file), capped at 1 MB with one rotation, at `logLevel` `error`, `info` (default) or `debug`. Every failure is logged with its context and stack; `info` adds sessions, commands, asks and their outcomes, quips, menu picks; `debug` adds question text, model result shapes and band decisions. It never holds your identity or anything from `~/.claude.json`.
- `/buddy log`: the log file's path and its last 20 lines, to paste into an issue.
- Test summaries of mocha, ava, tap, bun, minitest (`rake test`), deno, phpunit, ctest, dotnet test and Maven, of RSpec (`12 examples, 0 failures` passes; a non-zero failure count fails) and of Python unittest (`Ran N tests in Xs` then a line `OK`, `OK (skipped=1)` too, passes; a stray `OK` alone never counts) set off `testPass` and `testFail`.
- Quack has its own `petted` pose, eyes closed happy and a heart, and a `rest` pose: it floats on the water.

### Changed
- `/buddy list` and `/buddy use {id}` no longer ask the model: they reply with one line pointing to `/buddy-personality` (`Switching characters moved to /buddy-personality.`).
- The backup scan keeps real backup names only, skipping unfinished `.tmp.*` writes, empty files and files over 5 MB, and reads the newest 10.
- The id `original` is reserved for your original companion: a character file that takes it is refused with an error naming the file, never drawn; the error shows in the Your folder group of `/buddy-personality` and in the bubble at each session start and `/buddy reload`.
- An option that is ignored or capped is said once, in the first greeting's bubble, besides the log and `/buddy help`: never a silent revert. A characters folder that cannot be listed, or a file taking the reserved id, is said in the bubble at each session start and `/buddy reload`, pointing at `/buddy-personality`.
- The quick start leads with the `env` block of `settings.json`, which every new terminal keeps, with the shell export as the alternative, and says the `userConfig options not yet set` notice after install can be ignored: every option has a default.

The entries sit on the left in three titled groups, Shipped, Yours and Your folder, with `*` on the one drawn now; the right shows a live preview of the highlighted one: its sprite in its idle animation, its name, its description (for an original, its personality) and its greeting. ↑/↓ move the highlight and the preview follows; Enter switches to it and remembers it, across `/reload` and restarts; Esc closes the menu and changes nothing.
- Yours: the companion Claude Code's own `/buddy` hatched for your account before version 2.1.97 removed it. The menu reads `~/.claude.json` (never writes it), or the newest `~/.claude.json` backup that holds a companion, and lists it twice, as the native and the npm install rolled it: the same species, rarity, eyes, hat, shiny and stats, drawn with buddy's own art for all 18 species. A file that cannot be read or parsed is said in one line in the group, never shown as an empty one.
- An original companion wears its rarity's color; a shiny one cycles through the rainbow with a sparkle. Its preview and hover card show the stars, five stat bars and the day it hatched. Once picked, its name, personality and roll are saved, so a restart draws it without looking through backups; the menu marks it with `*` when it is drawn. The account id is never saved or logged.

- A short memory: the last `memory` exchanges (option, default 6, 0 = off, at most 30), each a `/buddy` question with its answer or a line its bubble showed on its own (a canned line or a quip, never the thinking filler), kept per session and per character across `/reload`, go before the question in its next answer or quip, so it can refer back to them; no extra model call.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-09-27

### Added
- A companion on the line above the Claude Code prompt: an ASCII character that walks, pauses to rest, stands still while Claude works, sleeps after midnight, and speaks in a one-line speech bubble. It draws in the terminal and the desktop app.
- Reactions: a failed or denied tool call, and test results in Bash output (pass or fail), change its pose and bring a line; a test pass sets off a short burst of confetti. Hovering the sprite shows a card with its name, description, pets, mood and the questions asked this session.
- `/buddy`: pet it (`/buddy`), list the characters (`/buddy list`), switch (`/buddy use {id}`, `/buddy use default`), hide and show (`/buddy off`, `/buddy on`), rescan the characters (`/buddy reload`), usage (`/buddy help`), and ask it anything else (`/buddy {question}`) for a one-line answer in character.
- Six built-in characters: `professor` (the default), `duck`, `cat`, `robot`, `ghost` and `dragon`.
- Your own characters: JSON files in the folder the `characterDir` option names, checked against `plugins/buddy/schema/character.schema.json`; an invalid one is listed with its first error, and choosing it draws the Professor with a bubble naming the error.
- Options `character`, `characterDir`, `motion`, `questionMode` (`fork`, `complete` or `off`), `quips` (off by default), `quipModel` and `quipCooldownSec`.
